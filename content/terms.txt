Terms of use

What LLM.Giving does
LLM.Giving helps volunteers direct unused AI subscription capacity toward useful deep research. You choose and operate your own AI service. LLM.Giving coordinates research requests, task packets, evidence and review; it does not run AI inference or sell access to an AI provider. Follow your provider's terms and usage restrictions.

Participation and publication
Submit only material you have authority to share. Do not include secrets, confidential information, sensitive personal information or material that facilitates harm. Topics require administrator approval. Once a topic is approved, valid task submissions can publish and advance research automatically. Disclosed human influence or uncertainty puts a packet on hold for administrator review. Files and other moderated material follow their own approval controls. The operator may restrict, remove, pause or restore material for safety, rights, privacy or integrity concerns.

Honest contributions
Paste the full handoff into an eligible AI, preserve its instructions and return the AI output unchanged. Disclose initial prompt edits, removed instructions, added sources, preferred conclusions and later steering. An unchanged platform formatting-repair prompt can be disclosed separately. Do not fabricate evidence, quotations, model identity, reasoning settings or reviewer independence. Passing a packet check is not submission; use the final receipt to confirm whether it was accepted or quarantined.

Permission to use submissions
For research requests and feedback you have authority to license, you grant the operator non-exclusive permission to store, display, distribute and incorporate that material into research context and service operation. Eligible original research and review contributions use CC BY 4.0. By submitting, you license the rights you hold under that license and designate the named contributors and LLM.Giving for attribution. Reuse of covered material requires appropriate credit, a link to the specific research result and license, and an indication of changes. Use the Cite this research button and follow the Licensing page. Third-party rights remain separate. We do not assume that every AI output has copyright protection or that you can grant rights you do not hold.

Research tool projects
Build a research tool projects retain source and tests as inert text. Original tool source and test code are offered under AGPL-3.0-only through a separate software consent. Original research narrative remains CC BY 4.0; third-party rights remain separate. Volunteers run and review tools in their own environments. Reproduction records and passing tests do not establish scientific correctness or a maintenance commitment.

Research limitations
Content is provided for research and information. It can be incomplete, inaccurate or unsafe. Acceptance checks establish required structure and evidence presence; they do not prove scientific truth. Review Complete and verified outcomes describe recorded contributor assessments. Task signatures and archived checksums do not authenticate an AI provider or prove that a conversation was unaltered. Assess claims independently before acting, especially for health, legal, financial or safety-critical decisions.

Management and removal
Keep your private management link secure. Anyone possessing it may manage the associated request. Withdrawal hides a request from public access; permanent deletion is a separate administrator action. Published copies, third-party reuse and backups may persist. Removing a page does not revoke CC BY 4.0 permissions already granted while its conditions are followed. See Privacy and Licensing for details.

Changes and concerns
The site records the policy version accepted with submissions. Later policy edits do not rewrite earlier acceptance records. Use research feedback for public scientific corrections and the private contact shown on this page for privacy, copyright or other sensitive concerns.
