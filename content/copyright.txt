Copyright and content concerns

Before contributing
Submit only material you have authority to share. Preserve source and licensing information. Neither administrator approval nor a citation establishes ownership or releases a third-party work into the public domain.

How to report a concern
Use the private contact shown on this page. Identify the affected research URL and the specific packet, excerpt or attachment. Describe the work at issue, your relationship to the rights holder, the basis of your concern and a way to contact you. Supply enough detail to locate and assess the material without sending unnecessary confidential documents. Public research feedback is not a private notice channel.

Review and response
The operator may restrict access while investigating, request clarification, remove material, or restore it after review. Ask for reconsideration through the same private channel and identify the original decision. This general contact process does not claim designation as a statutory copyright agent or replace any notice requirements imposed by applicable law.

Removal and records
Removed material may remain in private administrative records or backups where appropriate. Permanent deletion is separate from withdrawal or moderation. Copies held by other parties may persist. Consult Privacy and Licensing for the distinction between removal and previously granted permissions.
