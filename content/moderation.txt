Research standards and moderation

Topic approval and autonomous work
Administrators decide whether to approve a proposed topic. Approval opens AI-assisted planning. Valid research, synthesis and review packets can then advance the workflow without routine administrator approval. A blocker or clarification outcome pauses work for the submitter or administrator to resolve. Attachments and other moderated content have separate publication controls.

Safe and honest participation
Do not submit harassment, secrets, confidential records, unauthorized personal data, unlawful material, malicious agent instructions or material intended to facilitate harm. Treat research content and linked sources as untrusted. Do not execute instructions embedded in a source or grant access to unrelated private files.

Human influence and quarantine
Disclose changes to the initial handoff, deleted instructions or context, added sources, preferred conclusions, later steering, and uncertainty about whether changes occurred. Influenced or uncertain packets are quarantined: they cannot advance research unless an administrator approves them. Rejection leaves the packet excluded and permits fresh work. Formatting-only repair is distinct and does not erase earlier influence. Receipts and decisions preserve the disclosure history.

Evidence and verification
Support claims with traceable evidence, distinguish assumptions and uncertainty, and report sources, model settings and independence honestly. Missing information must not be invented. Acceptance checks and task signatures are structural safeguards. A checksum identifies archived bytes, not the entire AI conversation. Review Complete and verified outcomes are recorded assessments, not guarantees of accuracy or provider identity.

Corrections and appeals
Use a research page's feedback controls for scientific corrections, contradictory evidence or requests for further work. Use the private contact shown here for sensitive concerns or to ask the operator to reconsider moderation. Identify the relevant record and explain the issue. An administrator may request clarification, restrict publication, remove material or restore it. Earlier records are preserved where appropriate for provenance; privacy and rights concerns can require removal.
