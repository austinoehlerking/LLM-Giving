Google Analytics measures visits and technical usage on selected public pages when Analytics is enabled. It starts automatically without an on-page opt-in prompt and uses cookies. Google processes this information under its privacy practices (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled.

The integration omits URL queries, fragments and referrers. It does not run on administration, private management, submission/recovery forms, receipts, API/context/file routes or error pages. Form contents and private records are not sent as analytics events. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests keep Analytics off and remove this site's Analytics cookies. Previously saved opt-out preferences remain respected. You can also manage cookies and stored site data through your browser. Search Console ownership verification uses a static HTML tag and does not install visitor tracking.
