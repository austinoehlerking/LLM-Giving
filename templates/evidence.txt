TRUSTED RESEARCH EVIDENCE STANDARD — version 1
Research evidence must determine the strength and scope of conclusions. Use prior knowledge to guide investigation; label unverified claims. Do not work toward a preferred answer or treat earlier AI agreement as independent evidence.
Record actual searches and what you inspected, including failed searches, unavailable tools and abstract-only access. Never invent a source, quotation, search or access claim. Prefer relevant primary studies, original datasets and authoritative methods; judge quality and applicability instead of citation counts. Use a bounded stopping rule, not endless searching.
Return evidence with version=1, scope, stopping_rule, search_log, sources, claims and review_checks. Remove unused example entries; empty lists are valid where explained below. Planning tasks also need evidence_needs and stopping_rule per planned task.
search_log entries: query, where, result. Record actual actions and limitations. sources entries: unique id, title, URL (url; empty only for provided material), type (primary, dataset, review, official, provided, other), access (full_text, abstract_only, unavailable), access_note locating what was actually inspected. Source records and quotations are self-reported; the server does not fetch external pages.
Include EVERY central factual conclusion and numerical claim in claims, with a unique id and statement copied verbatim into body. The claim ID legacy is reserved for historical whole-result audits. Each claim needs kind (finding, inference, proposal), support (supported, limited, unresolved, unsupported), confidence (low, moderate, high), confidence_reason, limitations, counterevidence, derivation, basis_result_ids and links. Inferences/proposals must explain derivation. Cite original sources even when building on accepted task results; basis_result_ids identify current accepted work used, never historical invalidated results. Explain why contrary evidence does or does not change the conclusion, or honestly record the search gap. Separate observation, interpretation and proposed next steps. Preserve units, denominators, study conditions and uncertainty for numerical claims.
links entries: source_id, relation (supports, contradicts, context), locator (page/table/figure/section), a SHORT exact excerpt of inspected source text, explanation of relevance and limits. Full-source excerpts are limited to 1500 characters each; respect copyright and use the shortest sufficient passage. Unavailable sources have empty excerpts and cannot support a claim. Abstract-only access allows limited support. Supported/limited claims need inspected supporting links. High confidence requires supported evidence; unresolved claims require low confidence and explicit limits/next research steps. Unsupported claims cannot complete work. Do not conceal uncertainty to pass checks; a documented unresolved finding can complete a deliverable.
Reviewers must compare source passages to the precise claim, including scope, methods, units, uncertainty, contrary findings and inferential steps. Review source_checks record source_id, actual access, locator, a short exact excerpt (empty when unavailable), and assessment. A source being cited, an excerpt matching submitted text, or a valid signature establishes no scientific truth. Automated checks validate structure and references; scientific support remains an independent reviewer judgment.
OPTIONAL VISUAL RESEARCH AIDS: Use the trusted visual artifact format below to include evidence-linked charts, diagrams, step-based animations and interactive scenario pages in evidence.datasets/artifacts. Label conceptual, simulated and hypothetical behavior; retain assumptions, data provenance and limitations. Do not treat a visual as new evidence.
